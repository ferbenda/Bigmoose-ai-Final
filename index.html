FAQ

Real questions.
Honest answers.

New to AI? Perfect — most of our clients were too. These are the questions almost everyone asks us first.

What kind of businesses do you work with?
We work best with established businesses — companies that already have customers, a team and a real operation, and want to grow in a more organized way. If you're just starting out, we're probably not the right partner yet.
I don't know anything about AI. Is that a problem?
Not at all — that's exactly who we built this for. We explain everything in plain English, handle the technical side for you, and teach your team as we go. You never need to become an expert; that's our job.
What exactly do you do for my business?
Three things: we help you reach the right customers, we make your business easier to run, and we modernize how you look and communicate. You can start with just one — most clients do.
How much does it cost to get started?
Less than you'd think. We always start small, with one solution that can show results quickly. Once you see the return, you decide if and when to grow. You'll know the full cost upfront — no surprises, no hidden fees.
How fast will I see results?
Your first meeting happens within the week, and most clients see their first deliverables within 30 days. We prioritize quick wins first, so you see value early — not after six months.
Will AI replace my team?
No — it makes them better. AI takes over the repetitive, boring work so your people can focus on what humans do best: relationships, creativity and decisions. We also train your team, so they grow with the tools.
Do we need to meet in person, or is everything online?
Whatever works for you. We do video calls, and our team works across Miami, Madrid and Steamboat Springs when meeting face to face makes sense. Many clients mix both: online for the day-to-day, in person for the big moments.
I tried some AI tools and got overwhelmed. How is this different?
That's the most common story we hear. Tools alone don't help without a plan. We're not another tool — we're a partner who picks the right ones for you, sets them up, and walks you through everything from start to finish.
How will I know if it's actually working?
You'll see it in real time. We set up simple dashboards that show what matters: how much you spent, what came back, and what we're doing next. If something isn't performing, you'll hear it from us first — with a fix.
What makes you different from a regular marketing agency?
Regular agencies help you attract customers. We also improve what happens after — how you respond, follow up and operate. You get an expert team invested in your growth, not a vendor waiting for the next invoice.
